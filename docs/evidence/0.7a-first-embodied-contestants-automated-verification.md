## Failed PIE interaction check and correction — 6 September 2026

The Product Owner reported that the door, estate object and contestants could not be interacted with in the 0.7a PIE build. The shared defect was the third-person trace length: range validity was correctly measured from the pawn, but the ray started behind the pawn at the camera and used only the pawn-relative range, so the spring-arm separation consumed the usable ray.

The corrected trace adds camera-to-pawn separation to ray length while retaining pawn-relative authoritative range and first-hit visibility occlusion. A regression test now positions the camera more than 400 cm behind the pawn, traces against a real target inside pawn range and proves focus acquisition.

Development visuals were also replaced with composed built-in meshes: humanoid silhouettes, a framed hinged door with handle, and a pedestal-mounted estate seal. No downloaded or binary map assets were introduced.
