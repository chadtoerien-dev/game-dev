# Visual realism acceptance

Date: 16 September 2026
Status: required delivery gate; current build unscored

## Target

The user requests realistic AAA visual quality, referencing 007 First Light directionally, and requires at least 8/10. Evaluate the actual playable level, not generated concept art, selected marketing stills or intended settings. This rubric is a project judgement framework, not an objective industry certification or a claim of parity with another game.

## Scoring

Score each category from 0 to 10. Overall score = sum(category score × weight) / 100.

| Category | Weight | Inspect |
|---|---:|---|
| Architecture and environment | 20 | Believable scale, construction, spatial detail, source fidelity and documented deviations |
| Materials and surfaces | 15 | Appropriate response to light, scale, variation, contact and restrained wear |
| Lighting and exposure | 20 | Coherent illumination, shadowing, interiors/exteriors, exposure transitions and temporal stability |
| Characters | 15 | Anatomy, skin, hair, eyes, clothing, silhouettes and consistency across the cast |
| Animation and physical contact | 15 | Locomotion, turning, stopping, interaction, feet, hands, gaze and collision continuity |
| Camera and image stability | 10 | Gameplay framing, collision, clipping, aliasing, ghosting and readable motion |
| Presentation coherence | 5 | Consistent assets, effects and UI integration without disruptive placeholders |

Score anchors: 0 = absent; 2 = crude placeholder; 4 = recognisable prototype with pervasive artificial cues; 6 = credible mid-production work with frequent visible defects; 8 = convincing high-quality real-time presentation with minor defects only; 10 = exceptional consistency throughout the sampled gameplay with no material realism defects found. Scores of 8–10 require evidence in motion.

## Passing conditions

- Weighted overall score at least 8.0/10; every category at least 7.0/10.
- No major clipping, missing material, broken animation, floating character, severe temporal artefact or conspicuous placeholder on required routes.
- Performance passes separately on the agreed target machine and settings; a high-quality unplayable capture does not pass.
- Assess every required gameplay zone, all eight contestants, day and night, dialogue distance and ordinary traversal.
- Independent reviewer provides their own scores and defects before seeing the implementing agent's verdict where practical. Retain disagreements; do not average away a blocking defect.
- Both implementing and independent assessments must individually meet the overall and category thresholds. A lower independent score keeps the gate open for correction and reassessment; human acceptance does not silently override the requested numeric threshold.
- Chad accepts the final visible result. Agent self-assessment alone cannot approve production art.

## Capture protocol

Record build commit, package identity, date, hardware, resolution, scalability/upscaling settings and capture method. Retain representative stills and continuous gameplay footage: exterior arrival, interior traversal, close conversation, crowd/Tribunal, challenge motion, night operation and camera transitions. Include ordinary player camera distances and problematic angles. Match capture settings across revisions.

For each category retain score, evidence paths/timecodes, observed strengths, specific defects, severity and next corrective action. Record missing coverage as unassessed, never zero or a guessed passing score. The overall gate remains incomplete until all required categories and routes are assessed.
